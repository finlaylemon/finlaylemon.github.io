Mars Sample Retrieval Rover

Jan – Apr 2026

Course design project, team of three · SolidWorks, ROS 2, Nav2, Gazebo, RViz, Python

A rover concept for collecting the sample tubes cached at Mars's Three Forks Depot, built on the CSA Small Planetary Rover Platform (SPRP). I led the rover's design and did all of the CAD, including the sample retrieval system: a robotic arm with a claw and camera, a ten-tube sample bank, and deployable solar panels.

In a second phase, we implemented the software on provided rover hardware: ROS 2 on a Raspberry Pi 4, with an Arduino Uno driving the motors, using SLAM with LiDAR to map a room and navigate autonomously between points. The design report also covers surface operations, fault handling, and COSPAR planetary protection.

Desktop Wind Tunnels

Oct 2025 – 2026

Independent project · SolidWorks, FDM 3D printing, COTS integration

Two wind tunnels designed to be cheap to make and able to show airflow. The first is an open-return tunnel, built from 3D-printed and off-the-shelf parts as a low-cost alternative to the large commercial units used at Queen's. It is modular, so it prints in about 20 hours with household filament and broken parts can be swapped out. I designed a ridge-and-collar joint for it: printed collars snap around triangular ridges on each section and pull them tight, sealing the joint with a single bolt. It produced visible streamlines over a test airfoil.

The second is a closed-loop design twice the size, which recycles the exhaust air back to the fan for faster, more efficient flow. It was planned as a platform for comparison testing against an AGARD-B benchmark model, and was taken through CAD but not built.
